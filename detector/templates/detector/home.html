How the AI model works

1

Preprocess

Your image is converted to 3-channel grayscale, resized to 224×224 pixels and normalised with the same statistics the model was trained with.

2

Extract features

ResNet-18, pretrained on ImageNet, turns the image into 512 numbers describing edges, textures and shapes. Its last block was fine-tuned on chest X-rays.

3

Classify

A small final layer maps those 512 numbers to four scores, which a softmax converts into probabilities for COVID19, NORMAL, PNEUMONIA and TUBERCULOSIS.

4

Report

The class with the highest probability is shown, along with that probability. It is the model’s output score, not the chance that a person has COVID-19, pneumonia or tuberculosis.
